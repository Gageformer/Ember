9. ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Ember is an independent emulator, written from scratch for the PlayStation 2.
Its recompiler, memory model, geometry pipeline and graphics translation are
original work.

Where the PlayStation's hardware behaviour is subtle, the open-source emulator
DuckStation (https://github.com/stenzek/duckstation) was consulted as a
correctness reference -- a known-good implementation to compare measured
behaviour against. In a small number of components, individual routines are
currently derived from it, and those places are identified in Ember's source.
The intent is to replace them with original implementations over time.

Ember is not a fork or port of DuckStation, and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by it. Credit is given here in recognition of the value that project
has to everyone working in this space.
